Yaad knows when to decide, ask, and wait.

A bank transaction is an event. Spending is an interpretation. A receivable or expected refund is persistent state. Yaad connects those pieces without turning every payment into another chore.

Permission architecture

Understanding is not authority.

Yaad separates what it may see, understand, ask and act on. Payment authority is never inferred from data access.

SEEConsented accounts and commerce evidence.
UNDERSTANDEconomic meaning, context and obligations.
ASKClarify only when missing context materially changes the answer.
ACTIrreversible actions require an explicit, active grant.
