Unhappy paths

When evidence fails, Yaad becomes more conservative—not more confident.

VOICE UNCLEAR

Do not commit

Confirm the amount or fall back to text / tap.

CONSENT EXPIRED

Mark stale

Stop fresh retrieval and preserve the last authoritative state.

TWO REFUND MATCHES

Keep open

Do not force-match merely because the amounts coincide.

“I DON’T REMEMBER”

Preserve unresolved

Do not pressure the user or fabricate certainty.

NO ACTION AUTHORITY

Prepare, don’t execute

Understanding a payment never grants permission to move money.

Designed beyond the demo

The v1.1 rules generalise beyond the three recorded scenarios.

These are product cases the same evidence / confidence / obligation rules are designed to handle; they are not claims that every case was shown in the five-minute recording.

₹800 / ₹400 splitSame ₹1,200 payment; different user-confirmed shares.
Three-way splitValidate arithmetic before committing multiple receivables.
Partial reimbursementReduce the obligation without closing the remainder.
Loan repaymentMatch settlement to the prior loan obligation.
Partial refundKeep the residual merchant obligation open.
Duplicate / conflicting eventDeduplicate or preserve unresolved state rather than double-counting.
Permission change

Access can expand without silently granting action authority.

SEERead authorised financial evidenceON
UNDERSTANDInterpret economic meaningON
ASKRequest material clarificationON
ACTMove money or execute irreversible financial actionACT is OFF

A user can let Yaad see, understand and ask while keeping payment execution disabled. ACT requires a separate explicit grant.
