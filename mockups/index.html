Yaad — Human Interaction Map

Every point where a person sees, answers, authorises or corrects Yaad. Screens below are taken from the working Round 3 product wherever available; interaction-state mockups fill the two transient moments that occur inside the live flow.

SEE — understand stateASK — clarify only when materialREMEMBER — persist contextACT — explicit authority only
Design principle: Yaad should consume less attention, not more. If evidence is sufficient, the best interaction is no interaction.
01 · Home

Home — “Your month makes sense.”

The primary surface. It shows explained spending, Money Due Back, transfers excluded, runway, watching and whether anything Needs You.

02 · Needs You

Needs You — one material ambiguity

Only a payment whose meaning could materially change spending or an obligation is escalated.

03 · Voice clarification

Voice clarification — ask once, naturally

Vikram can answer by voice or text. Voice is treated as evidence, not automatically as financial truth.

05 · Clarification result

Clarification result — ₹600 spent, ₹600 due back

The confirmed meaning becomes Financial Memory: a shared expense with an open receivable from Arjun Kumar.

06 · Money Due Back

Money Due Back — obligations remain open

Arjun’s ₹600 receivable and UrbanStep’s ₹3,499 refund remain visible until credible settlement evidence closes them.

07 · Transactions

Transactions — movement separated from meaning

Raw movement and economic meaning stay distinct. A self-transfer can appear as movement while remaining excluded from spending.

08 · Context Layer

Context Layer — payment plus economic meaning

The proposed Financial Context Layer preserves purpose, personal share, receivable, counterparty, source, confidence and obligation state alongside the payment reference.

09 · Memory

Memory — confirmed context compounds

Confirmed relationships reduce future maintenance, but remembered patterns never override contradictory present evidence.

10 · Permissions

Permissions — SEE / UNDERSTAND / ASK / ACT

Access to data is separated from interpretation, clarification and irreversible action authority.

11 · Ask Yaad

Ask Yaad — voice or text

A person can proactively explain a payment or answer a clarification without navigating through transaction-management screens.

12 · Financial Memory

Financial Memory — relationships, not rows

The user sees accounts, people and obligations as connected financial state rather than isolated transaction lines.

13 · Stale / revoked / incomplete data

Stale or revoked access — uncertainty stays visible

If consent expires or retrieval becomes partial, Yaad preserves the last authoritative state, stops fresh retrieval and marks affected memory stale/partial rather than pretending it is complete.
