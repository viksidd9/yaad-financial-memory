05 · Transactions

Transactions — movement separated from meaning

Raw movement and Yaad’s economic interpretation remain distinct. A self-transfer can exist as movement while being excluded from spending.

06 · Money Due Back

Money Due Back — obligations remain open

UrbanStep’s ₹3,499 refund and Arjun’s ₹600 receivable stay visible until credible settlement evidence closes them.

07 · Context Layer

Context Layer — payment plus economic meaning

The proposed Financial Context Layer preserves purpose, personal share, receivable, counterparty, source, confidence and obligation state alongside the payment reference.

08 · Memory

Memory — confirmed context compounds

Confirmed relationships reduce future maintenance, while contradictory fresh evidence always wins.

09 · Permissions

Permissions — SEE / UNDERSTAND / ASK / ACT

Data access, interpretation, clarification and irreversible action authority are separated explicitly.

10 · Ask Yaad

Ask Yaad — voice or text

A person can proactively explain a payment or answer a clarification without maintaining a manual ledger.

11 · Financial Memory

Financial Memory — relationships, not rows

The user sees accounts, people and obligations as connected financial state rather than isolated transaction lines.
